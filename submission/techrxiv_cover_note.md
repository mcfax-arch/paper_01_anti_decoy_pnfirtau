# Cover note for TechRxiv submission

Dear TechRxiv Team,

I am submitting the preprint **"Attention with a Calibrated Off Switch: Causal FIR Key Smoothing, the Score-Shift Pathology, and Selective Silence in Rectified Attention (pn_fir_tau)"** for consideration as a preprint.

The manuscript is an empirical and algorithmic study of attention normalization under causal key smoothing. It contains 21 pages, 18 figures, and 12 tables, including a reproducibility package with source code, experiment suites, raw JSON results, and the figure-generation pipeline. The study reports both positive results and negative findings; all quantitative claims are traced to the included measurement record.
