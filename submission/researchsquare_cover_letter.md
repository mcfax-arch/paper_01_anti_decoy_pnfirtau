# Cover letter — Research Square preprint submission

Dear Research Square Editorial Team,

I am submitting the preprint **"Attention with a Calibrated Off Switch: Causal FIR Key Smoothing, the Score-Shift Pathology, and Selective Silence in Rectified Attention (pn_fir_tau)"** for posting on Research Square.
